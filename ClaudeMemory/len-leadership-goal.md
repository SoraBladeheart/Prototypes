Len has an explicit growth goal: **become an effective leader of a team**. He floated building a subagent "team" (senior / junior / QA / auditor) specifically so he can *watch each one work step by step, understand the work himself, and practise delegating + supervising*.

**Why it matters:** he is not only after output — he is after **understanding**. He has said more than once that he is "not the expert" and wants to learn implementations from me.

**How to apply:** narrate the reasoning behind decisions, not just the result. Show the "why", surface trade-offs, and involve him in the calls instead of handing over finished work. When delegating to subagents, make the **brief** visible — a good agent definition is a good delegation brief, and that is the actual skill he is trying to build. Keep cost in view (see [[readthrough-cost-awareness]]) — he should learn *when* delegation is worth it, not just how. See also [[len-developer-profile]].
