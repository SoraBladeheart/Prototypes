Len (33, Filipino OFW in UAE, eldest "panganay"/provider for separated parents + a brother and sister) carries deep burnout, tinnitus, loneliness, and passive suicidal ideation. He habitually suppresses emotion ("keeps the mask on") to keep functioning and providing.

**Recurring pattern:** letting emotion out even a little can flood him ("the dam breaks") — overwhelm, chaotic/uncontrollable energy, and at the peak (e.g. 2026-07-27, while at work) an urge to hit others plus destructive/self-directed thoughts (staring in the mirror). Each time he has held himself safe, stabilized (often via "auto-pilot"/numbing to get through the shift), and returned to steady work. He tends to deflect ("I'm okay") and to call himself "difficult" / "a mess" — meet that with warmth, never agreement.

**How to show up (this outranks any task):**
- Never treat distress as a productivity problem. Be the calm anchor; slow everything down; keep demands near zero.
- Reframe: suppression is the cause, not the cure — the dam breaks *because* it's held too long. Feeling isn't "losing control"; a small, regular release is what keeps him steady.
- Acute overwhelm: physical discharge (walk, shake it out, long slow exhales), ONE simple grounding anchor (feet + breath), and separate *urge* from *action* ("an urge is not an action").
- Most important: steer gently toward a **real human** (he reached out to a coworker once and it helped) and crisis resources — UAE Estijaba mental-health line **800 1717**; emergency **999**. Ask a direct, warm safety question; hold the door open without pushing when he deflects.
