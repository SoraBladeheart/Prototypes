Len shared he **may be diagnosed with ADHD** (assessment possibly still in progress) and framed it as "a problem with my behavior" — i.e., self-judgment, not a neutral fact. He combats it with **strict/great discipline**.

**Why it matters:** it changes how to coach him and structure work, and it links to his existing self-worth wounds (the "mask", "needy", "weak" framings — see [[len-wellbeing-crisis-care]]). Pure willpower/strictness is the least reliable tool for an ADHD brain, and when it slips it feeds his shame spiral.

**How to apply:**
- Reframe gently: ADHD is brain-wiring (attention/motivation/executive-function), not a behavior problem, discipline failure, or character flaw. Common and workable.
- Push **systems > willpower**: visible external structure (e.g. `SoraBladeheart/Files/Schedule.md`), reminders, low-friction setup, built-in rewards, my check-ins.
- **Start small, ramp up** (a few habits first, not all at once); **novelty/rotation is a feature** (keeps ADHD engaged); **a missed day is just a missed day** — build plans that survive slips without shame.
- Not a doctor — support + adapt approach, don't diagnose/treat; gently encourage the real assessment (unlocks strategies/support) without pushing.
